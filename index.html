Before you ask

The questions you were about to ask.

If you're a brand

What does it cost?

You get one fixed price in writing before anyone films. It depends on what you want made, how long you want to use it and whether you want the creator to stay away from your competitors. Send the brief and you'll get a number back.

What if the video doesn't do well?

Nobody can promise views, and anyone who does is guessing. Ads usually get fewer views than a creator's normal posts. That's true of everyone. What we do is show you a typical post and not the best one, and you see the video before it goes up.

Can we run the video as our own ad?

Yes, if we agree it. That's priced on its own and written down with dates, so nobody is arguing about it in three months.

How long have you been going?

Since 2026, with one creator. That's on purpose. Your brief goes to the founder and gets a reply within one working day, usually within the hour.

We're an agency booking for a client. Is that fine?

Yes. Same process, same speed.

How do we pay?

By invoice, on the terms we agree before filming starts.

If you're a creator

Do I even need a manager?

Maybe not. If the deals are coming in and you don't mind the emails, the contracts and the chasing, carry on. If that's eating the time you'd spend making videos, that's the bit we take.

What do you charge?

A cut of the deals we find or close for you. Nothing up front and no monthly fee. If you don't get paid, we don't either. The exact cut is agreed in writing before we start.

Am I locked in?

No. The agreement is one page in plain English, and either of us can end it with notice.

What about brands I already work with?

They stay yours. We only take a cut where we found the deal or closed it.

Do you decide what I post?

No. Nothing goes ahead unless you say yes.

When do I get paid?

Brands often pay 30 to 60 days after the post goes up. We chase it so you're not the one sending the awkward email.

Which side are you on?

Two minutes. Tell us what you need and you'll hear back from Yacine within one working day, usually within the hour.
